Landlord register deadline in South East

If you rent out a home in South East, you must add it to the new PRS Database (the national landlord register) by 14 June 2027.

Register opens15 March 2027
Deadline14 June 2027
Fee£65 / property / year

Who this applies to

Private landlords with rented homes anywhere in South East, including Brighton and Hove, Southampton, Portsmouth, Reading, Oxford, Milton Keynes, Slough, Canterbury, Maidstone, Guildford, Crawley, Basingstoke, High Wycombe, Medway. The date follows the property's location, not where you live.

What to do and when

  1. Now: find your gas safety record, EICR (electrical report) and EPC. Note their dates. Book any that have expired.
  2. Before 15 March: gather tenancy details: occupants, rent, bills included, any licence numbers.
  3. From 15 March: register each property on the official GOV.UK service and pay £65 per property.
  4. By 14 June: every let property in South East should have an active entry. After this councils can enforce.

Questions landlords in South East ask

When must landlords in South East register on the PRS Database?

The regulations start in South East on 15 March 2027. You then have three months, so the deadline is 14 June 2027. You can register early.

How much does it cost?

Published local-authority guidance says £65 per property per year. The fee is per property, not per landlord, and is paid again on each annual renewal.

What happens if I don't register?

Councils can issue a civil penalty of up to £7,000 for letting or advertising without an active entry, and up to £40,000 for repeated breaches or false information. Unregistered landlords are also limited in the possession grounds they can use.

What do I need before I start?

Your contact details, the property address and type, number of bedrooms and occupants, rent and included bills, any HMO or selective licence number, your gas safety record date (if there is gas), your EICR expiry date and your EPC details.

Does it depend on where I live?

No. It depends on where the rented property is. A landlord living elsewhere with a home in South East follows the South East dates.
