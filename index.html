    Questions landlords ask

    Who has to register?

    Private landlords in England with assured or regulated tenancies, for each rented home. Letting agents can help, but the duty is the landlord's.

    What happens if I don't?

    Councils can issue civil penalties of up to £7,000, rising to £40,000 for repeated or false entries. Unregistered landlords also face limits on possession grounds and possible rent repayment orders of up to 24 months.

    What will the register ask for?

    Your contact details; the property address, type and bedrooms; occupants and any HMO or licence numbers; rent and included utilities; gas safety record date, EICR expiry and EPC details.

    Is this the official service?

    No. LandlordReady is an independent tool. Register on the official GOV.UK service. Dates and fees here are based on published guidance and may change, so always check GOV.UK.
